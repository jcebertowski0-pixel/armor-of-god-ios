# App Review 2.1 recording checklist (physical device)

Apple asked for a screen recording on a physical device running the latest iOS.
This is the script Jacob set. Nothing here is evidence until someone actually films it.

1. 30 seconds of opening the app from the home screen.
2. Sign up flow: create an account with email and password, show the OTP entry step.
3. Sign in with the demo account from the App Review Notes.
4. Walk each main tab: Home, Heart, Chef, Lift, More.
5. RoundTable: post a message and show it appearing; report and block a post.
6. Account deletion: Settings, delete account, confirm.
7. Shield and Guide: tap both, show the Coming soon badge and that nothing opens.
8. End recording. One continuous take, latest iOS, under four minutes.

Blocker: I cannot film a physical iPhone. If the owner cannot either, say so in the notes
and offer this checklist as the test method. Never claim a recording exists unless one does.
